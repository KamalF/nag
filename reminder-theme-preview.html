Patina structure, Horizon palette

Faded upcoming rows, pill actions, italic serif times, generous spacing — on slate rather than warm bone. One dark theme, one light. The second row in each is shown mid-flash, which is what a row does when you click its notification.

What changed from the two originals

  • Amber can't just invert: #E0A33E on light slate lands near 2:1, so the light theme uses #96620F (4.6:1). Same token name, different value.
  • Light "quiet" ink went to #5C6C72 rather than the #67787E I proposed — that one measured 4.05:1, under the floor. Dark quiet ink sits at 5.4:1.
  • Patina's fade is doing real work here: upcoming rows drop to quiet ink, overdue rows get full ink, a lifted ground, and a 2px amber spine. Nothing else uses amber.
  • Zone names are voiced, not labelled — "Needs you now" and "Waiting quietly" instead of Overdue / Upcoming.
  • Chips carry no digit hints and no pressed state: one click saves, so there is nothing to select and nothing to confirm.
  • The last quiet row shows a [label](url) link rendered inline. Links take ink, never amber — amber means late and nothing else.
  • The Dark / Light / Side-by-side buttons are preview chrome. The app has no theme switcher: tokens sit on :root with a prefers-color-scheme override, nothing is persisted, and nothing sets data-theme. It is scoped that way here only so both palettes fit on one page.
