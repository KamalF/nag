Hours

Calendar + wheels

Month grid for the date, snap-scrolling wheels for the time. Hours obey the configured range, minutes are quarters only, and it opens on 9:00. Change the range above to see the wheel rebuild.

  • The wheels are real scroll containers with scroll-snap-type: y mandatory — thumb-flickable, and each value is also a button so it works by tap and by keyboard.
  • Selection is read from scroll position, so there's no drag maths to get wrong and no library.
  • Past days stay tappable, just dimmed — backdating is occasionally what you want. It lands in "Needs you now" and does not notify: anything over 15 minutes late is marked and skipped (SPEC §7.3), so the summary line says exactly that rather than promising a ping.
  • The hour wheel can't leave the configured range, so there is no "outside your usual hours" hint to show.
  • Both toggles at the top are preview chrome — the app has no theme switcher (it follows prefers-color-scheme, tokens on :root) and no way to widen the hour range from the sheet. They exist here so one page can show every palette and every configured range.
  • The chosen wall-clock time is read in the browser's timezone and sent as an absolute instant; the server stores UTC and resolves presets in its own zone (SPEC §6).
  • Config this maps onto:
[picker]
hour_min     = 8
hour_max     = 18
minute_step  = 15        # 00 / 15 / 30 / 45
default_time = "09:00"
week_start   = "monday"
